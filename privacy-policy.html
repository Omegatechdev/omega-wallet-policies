Privacy Policy for Omega Wallet

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Omega Wallet ("the app") is a personal budgeting app published by Abdul Basit, publishing as Omega Tech Developers ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, where it is stored, and how you can remove it.

By using Omega Wallet you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account information. To register you provide three things: your name, your email address and a password. Nothing else is asked for — no profile photo, no phone number, no date of birth. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your password in hashed form; we never see or store your password in readable text.

Your name is used to address you inside the app and on your reports. Your email address identifies your account, lets you reset your password, and lets us reply if you contact support.

Financial information you enter. The app stores the budget data you type into it, including:

  • Income sources and the expenses recorded against each of them
  • General expenses such as utilities, food, clothing and similar categories
  • Cashbook entries: amounts given or received, the name and phone number of the person or party involved, dates and notes
  • Your selected currency and app preferences

This information is entered by you. We do not connect to your bank, card or any financial institution, and we do not import transactions automatically.

Contact information you choose to add. A cashbook entry has an optional phone number field. You can leave it blank, type a number manually, or import one contact from your device's contact list so that you do not have to type it. If you import a contact, the app reads only the name and phone number of that single contact you tapped, and saves it inside that one cashbook entry in your account.

Contacts access is entirely optional. If you deny the permission, every part of the app keeps working — you can still create cashbook entries, generate reports and use every feature; you just type the number yourself or leave the field empty.

We do not upload, copy, index, transmit or store your contact list. Contacts you do not select are never read by us, and the app does not access contact emails, addresses, photos or any other field. We never read contacts in the background, and nothing is read unless you open the picker and choose someone.

Technical information. We may collect basic diagnostic data such as device model, operating system version, app version and crash reports through Google Firebase services, so that we can fix errors and improve stability.

We do not collect your location, photos, camera, microphone, SMS messages or call logs. We do not read your contact list in bulk.

Permissions the app uses

  • Contacts — used only when you tap to import a contact into a cashbook entry, so you do not have to retype the number. Fully optional: you may deny it, type the number yourself, or leave the phone number field blank. The app's features do not depend on it.
  • Internet / network state — to sync your records with your Firebase account.
  • Storage (on older Android versions) — to save the PDF reports you generate to your device.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in
  • To save your budget records and sync them to your device
  • To generate the PDF reports you request from your cashbook
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app's performance
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not use your financial records for advertising or share them with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore / Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google LLC on its secure cloud infrastructure. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by Firebase security rules so that a signed-in user can only read and write their own records.

Google's handling of data processed through Firebase is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Omega Wallet uses the following third-party services, which may process limited data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in and account management
  • Cloud Firestore / Firebase Realtime Database — storage of your budget records
  • Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics — crash reports and basic usage statistics, where enabled

PDF reports and sharing

Cashbook PDF reports are created on your device. When you tap share, Android's standard share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes — WhatsApp, email, Google Drive, a printer, or local storage. The file is handed to the app you pick; it does not pass through our servers, and we have no record of what you shared or with whom.

Once a report leaves the app it is governed by the privacy policy of the service you sent it to, and by whoever receives it. Because a ledger report contains names, phone numbers and amounts, share it only with people entitled to see it.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: open the Profile tab, tap Delete account and confirm.

Deleting your account permanently removes your income sources, expenses, cashbook entries (including any names and phone numbers saved in them), preferences and authentication record from our Firebase project. Deleted data cannot be recovered, so export any PDF reports you want to keep before you delete.

Full details, including how to request deletion by email if you can no longer sign in, are on our Account and Data Deletion page.

Security

We rely on Google Firebase's encryption in transit and at rest, together with per-user access rules, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password confidential and use the screen lock on your device.

Children's privacy

Omega Wallet is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can access and edit your records at any time inside the app, export your cashbook as a PDF, and delete your account and data. If you are in a region covered by the GDPR, the CCPA or a similar law, you may also request a copy of your data or object to its processing by contacting us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app or on the Google Play listing.
