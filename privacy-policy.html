Privacy Policy

Omega Wallet · Effective 5 November 2026

How Omega Wallet handles your personal data.

Information we collect

To provide the App’s features, we collect the information you give us directly: your name, email address and optional contact number. We also collect the financial records you create: income sources, expenses, cashbook entries, categories, and any notes or descriptions you enter. If you use the “import from contacts” option in Cashbook, we access your device’s contacts only to fill in the name and number of the contact you choose.

How we use your information

We use your information to manage your financial records, generate summaries and reports, improve app performance, fix bugs and technical issues, and provide customer support.

Data storage

Your information is stored securely using Google Firebase, a cloud platform provided by Google. Your financial records and account information are stored in Firebase databases to enable secure access and backup across your devices. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information.

Biometric login

If you enable Face ID or fingerprint login, verification is performed entirely by your device’s operating system. We never receive, transmit, or store your biometric data ourselves.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial information. We may share it only when required by law, to protect legal rights, or with trusted infrastructure providers, such as our cloud database and storage provider, that help operate the App, subject to appropriate safeguards. To show ads, limited device data (such as your advertising ID) is shared with Google AdMob, as described under Advertising. Your income, expense and cashbook records are never shared with advertisers.

Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • Ad interaction data, such as impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information

This may be used to personalize ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. We do not give advertisers your name, email address, or any financial data you enter in the App.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings. On Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads. On iOS, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Data security

We use industry-standard security measures to help protect your information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access or correct your information and withdraw consent where applicable. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile in the App, and export your Cashbook ledger as a PDF statement from any party’s detail screen.

Data retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations. If you delete your account, your records are removed from our systems and any backups age out within 30 days.

Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third-party services

The App relies on Google Firebase for authentication, database and file storage, on Google Sign-In for account access, and on Google AdMob for advertising. Each provider has its own privacy practices governing the data it processes on our behalf.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
