Delete your Omega Wallet account and data

App: Omega Wallet · Developer: Abdul Basit, publishing as Omega Tech Developers · Last updated: 12 September 2026

You can delete your Omega Wallet account and everything stored with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Omega Wallet and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked. If you are prompted for your password, enter it so we can verify it is you.

Your account and records are removed immediately and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in, send an email to omegadevelopers47@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line Delete my Omega Wallet account. We verify the request and complete the deletion within 7 days, and we will reply to confirm once it is done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account and sign-in record (name, email address, password credential, authentication ID)Deleted permanently
Income sources and the expenses recorded against themDeleted permanently
General expenses (utilities, food, clothing and similar)Deleted permanently
Cashbook entries: names, phone numbers, amounts given and received, dates, notesDeleted permanently
Currency choice and app preferencesDeleted permanently
Anonymous crash and diagnostic logsNot linked to your account; automatically discarded by Firebase within its standard retention window (up to 90 days)
PDF reports you already exported or shared to WhatsApp, email or DriveStay on your device or wherever you saved them; delete those yourself

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Export any cashbook PDF reports you want to keep before you delete your account.

Retention

We do not keep a backup copy of deleted accounts. Once deletion is processed there is no waiting or grace period and no archived copy to restore.

Contact

For help with deletion or any question about your data: omegadevelopers47@gmail.com
